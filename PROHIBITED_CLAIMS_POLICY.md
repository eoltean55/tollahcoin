# Prohibited claims policy

This policy keeps tollahcoin communications factual and reviewable.

## Do not claim

- Guaranteed returns.
- Guaranteed exchange listings.
- Guaranteed liquidity.
- Guaranteed safety.
- Guaranteed price targets.
- "Next 100x" or similar performance claims.
- "Audited" unless an audit exists and is linked.
- "Liquidity locked" unless a lock exists and is linked.
- "Ownership renounced" unless the live contract proves it.
- "Verified" unless PolygonScan verification is complete.

## Current-events rule

The project may discuss culture and identity, but it should not use war, violence, fear, or crisis urgency to pressure people to buy.

Allowed:

> `$IRAN` is a Polygon community token built around culture, identity, transparent token details, and a verifiable buy path.

Not allowed:

> Buy now because of the war.

## Community rule

No spam, bot raids, mass direct messages, fake testimonials, fake support accounts, or copied posts across rooms that do not allow project promotion.

## Review rule

Before posting a major claim, ask:

1. Is it true today?
2. Can a stranger verify it from a public link?
3. Would it still be accurate if quoted by an exchange reviewer?

If the answer is no, do not post it.
