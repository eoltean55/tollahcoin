Swap checklist

1. Prepare wallet

Use a self-custody wallet that supports Polygon. Keep a small amount of `MATIC` for gas.

2. Verify first

Paste the contract yourself or use the official link. Never trust screenshots, DMs, or copied ticker symbols.

3. Review the swap

Check liquidity, price impact, slippage, and route before confirming. Small pools can move quickly.

Small-liquidity warning

If the first pool starts with a few hundred dollars, it is proof-of-market liquidity. Slippage may be high, price can move sharply, and large buys or sells may fail or execute poorly.
