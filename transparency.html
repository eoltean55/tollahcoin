Before major promotion

Contract proof

  • Verify source code on PolygonScan.
  • Publish taxes, minting, pause, blacklist, and owner powers.
  • Add tests if the contract is managed in this repo.

Liquidity proof

  • Publish pool address and pair.
  • Show whether liquidity is unlocked or locked.
  • Warn when pool depth is small.

Listing proof

  • Keep website, socials, logo, and contract consistent.
  • Submit metadata to token lists and wallets.
  • Apply to trackers only after chart and liquidity data exist.

Exchange data room checklist

Project facts

Name, ticker, website, contract, chain, logo, official socials, and contact email.

Market facts

DEX pool, liquidity depth, volume, holder count, chart links, and token-list links.

Compliance facts

Legal structure, risk disclosures, owner wallet, treasury policy, and prohibited claims policy.
