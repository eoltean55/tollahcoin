# Risk disclosure

tollahcoin (`$IRAN`) is a high-risk crypto asset. This project should be presented with clear risk language before any major marketing, liquidity, or listing outreach.

## Core risks

- Token prices can move sharply and may go to zero.
- Liquidity may be small, especially at launch.
- Small pools can create high slippage and failed swaps.
- Smart contracts can contain bugs even when tested.
- Wallets, DEXs, bridges, and RPC providers can fail.
- Listings on trackers, wallets, or exchanges are not guaranteed.
- Laws, regulations, sanctions rules, and platform policies can change.

## No financial advice

Nothing in this repository, website, data room, social content, or community chat is financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.

## Buyer responsibility

Anyone interacting with `$IRAN` should:

- Verify the contract address independently.
- Review the verified source code after deployment.
- Understand pool liquidity and slippage.
- Use only funds they can afford to lose.
- Ignore direct messages, fake support accounts, fake contracts, and pressure to buy quickly.

## Project communication standard

The project should not promise returns, safety, exchange listings, liquidity locks, audits, or future market performance unless those facts are publicly linked and verifiable.
