# Community outreach playbook

This project can use X and chat rooms to share information, but it should not spam, mislead people, or exploit war or crisis. The goal is education, transparency, and community formation.

## Positioning

Use this simple frame:

> `$IRAN` is a Polygon community token built around culture, identity, transparent token details, and a verifiable buy path.

Avoid:

- "Guaranteed gains"
- "Next 100x"
- "Exchange listing confirmed"
- "Buy before it is too late"
- War hype, violent language, harassment, or dehumanizing political claims

## Channel plan

### X

- Create an official project account.
- Pin the website, contract address, PolygonScan link, buy guide, and risk note.
- Post progress updates when facts change: verified contract, pool created, chart page live, token-list submission, liquidity lock.
- Use short educational threads instead of only memes.

### Telegram or Discord

- Use one official chat room first.
- Pin the contract, website, rules, and scam warning.
- Add moderators before broad promotion.
- Ban impersonators, fake support DMs, and price-manipulation spam.

### Existing crypto chat rooms

- Join only rooms where project sharing is allowed.
- Read each room's rules before posting.
- Answer questions transparently.
- Do not mass-post the same message across many rooms.
- Do not DM strangers asking them to buy.

### Liquidity update

### Current-events-safe message

> Current events have more people talking about Iranian identity and culture. `$IRAN` is our community token around that conversation, but we will not use fear, violence, or crisis hype to push buys. Verify everything and make your own decision.

## Daily operating rhythm

- 1 transparency post.
- 1 education post.
- 1 community prompt.
- 1 reply session where Elliott answers questions.
- 0 fake engagement, bot raids, or spam blasts.

## Moderator rules

- Remove scams, fake contracts, fake support links, and impersonators.
- No promises of profit.
- No hate, harassment, or violent rhetoric.
- No pressure campaigns telling people they must buy.
- Keep the official contract and links pinned.

## What Cursor will not do
