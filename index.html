Legitimacy checklist

Make the token easy to verify.

These are the practical steps that make $IRAN easier to trust, easier to list, and easier to buy.

Contract controls

  • Verify ERC-20 source code on PolygonScan.
  • Publish mint, tax, blacklist, pause, and owner permissions.
  • Use multisig ownership or explain renounced ownership clearly.

Token facts

  • Publish total supply, decimals, allocations, and liquidity policy.
  • Share one canonical contract address everywhere.
  • Document risk plainly before major marketing begins.

Usability

  • Pin a verified buy guide with DEX and explorer links.
  • Submit consistent logo metadata to wallets and token lists.
  • Add official chart, pool, and social links as they go live.

Exchange readiness

Earn listings with clean data.

Exchange access starts with a trustworthy DEX route, then consistent public data for trackers, wallets, and future centralized exchanges.

1. DEX liquidity

Create a reputable Polygon pool, publish the pool address, show chart links, and keep the official swap link consistent.

2. Trackers and wallets

Apply to CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, Dexscreener, GeckoTerminal, and wallet metadata lists once liquidity and socials are stable.

3. CEX data room

Prepare legal notes, tokenomics, owner controls, liquidity proof, social links, logo assets, and risk disclosures before outreach.
