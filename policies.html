Public policies

Trust starts with clear rules.

These policies define how tollahcoin should talk about risk, treasury funds, liquidity, and claims before deployment, liquidity, and listing outreach.

Policy set

Risk disclosure

Crypto is high risk. Buyers must verify the contract, understand liquidity, and avoid pressure to buy.

Open risk policy

Treasury policy

Project funds should be separated, tracked, and used only for project operations, liquidity, and contributors.

Open treasury policy

Prohibited claims

No guaranteed returns, fake listings, fake liquidity, war-pressure buying, spam, or unverified safety claims.

Open claims policy

Rules before major outreach

Verify first

Do not claim deployed, verified, locked, audited, or listed status until public links prove it.

Keep records

Track treasury transfers, pool creation, liquidity changes, listing submissions, and contributor payments.

Stay factual

Marketing can be energetic, but claims must stay accurate, non-coercive, and reviewable.

Policies should be updated when live project facts change.
