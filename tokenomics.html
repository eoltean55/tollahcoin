Draft tokenomics

Simple rules are easier to trust.

Tokenomics means the token's rules and numbers: supply, fees, ownership, allocations, liquidity, and what can change later.

Not final until the contract matches

This page is a starter disclosure template. Do not market these as final facts until the deployed contract, pool, and owner wallet prove them on-chain.

Allocation disclosure to fill in

Liquidity

Publish the percentage and wallet used to seed the first pool, then link the live pool address.

Treasury

Publish the owner or treasury wallet so holders can verify project funds and spending.

Community

Publish any rewards, airdrops, or contributor allocations before distributing them.

Claims to avoid

No profit promises

Do not promise returns, floors, targets, or exchange-driven price movement.

No fake safety claims

Do not say liquidity is locked, ownership is renounced, or code is audited unless linked proof exists.

No hidden controls

Publish taxes, minting, pause, blacklist, and owner permissions in plain English.

Tokenomics must match the verified contract and public wallets.
